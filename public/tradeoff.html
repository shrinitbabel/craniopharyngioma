Craniopharyngioma.AI · Decision Support

Every resection trades recurrence against permanent DI.

Craniopharyngioma surgery is a balance: cut more and the tumor is less likely to return, but the hypothalamic–pituitary axis is more likely to fail — leaving the patient with lifelong diabetes insipidus. This tool makes that balance quantitative and patient-specific, using two explainable ensemble models to trace where each surgical choice lands.

Explainability · SHAP

Why the two risks move in opposite directions.

Kernel SHAP attributes each model’s output to its inputs across the cohort. Extent of resection is a top-ranked driver of both outcomes — which is exactly why it is a lever, and why pulling it helps one risk while hurting the other.
